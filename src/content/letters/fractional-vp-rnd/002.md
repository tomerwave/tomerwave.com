The market finally admits what early teams already feel. Fractional CTO and fractional VP Engineering are not a compromise brand. They are how a lot of Series A companies get senior pushback without waiting half a year for a full-time hire that might not stick.

The useful distinction this week is still the boring one. Strategy facing the board is not the same job as running the team that ships. If your pain is incidents, hiring, and a founder who cannot leave the sprint, you do not need another vision deck. You need someone who will say no inside the week.

The essay walks through what to do before you can justify a VP of R&D seat, including the checks that tell you fractional is enough for now.

> Tip: if you cannot name the decisions you want challenged in the next thirty days, you are shopping for a title, not for leadership.
