Early teams do not only lack a VP R&D. They lack pushback.

Rewrites, multi-year tools, and "obvious" platform bets sail through because the room sounds confident and disagreeing is expensive. Bad decisions happen everywhere. The expensive pattern is bad decisions with no opposing memo.

This month's essay is the pre-hire kit: a forced counter-case on irreversible decisions, a red team hour that is not optional, a narrow external sparring day when needed, and interview habits that reward people who have killed work before. It is deliberately not another "when to hire" piece.

> Tip: if nobody will write the counter-case, you do not have a template problem. You have a culture (or power) problem, and a VP alone will not invent challenge from zero.

When you want a plain read of whether the gap is challenge, ownership, or an actual hire, the R&D health check is built for that middle.
