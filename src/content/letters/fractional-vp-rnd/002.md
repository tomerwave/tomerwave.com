Early teams do not only lack a VP R&D. They lack pushback.

Rewrites, multi-year tools, and "obvious" platform bets sail through because the room sounds confident and disagreeing is expensive. Bad decisions happen everywhere. The expensive pattern is bad decisions with no opposing memo.

This week Google is talking about every engineer becoming a team lead of agents, and the industry is racing to put agents in every app. That only works if someone still writes the counter-case before you commit.

> If nobody will write the counter-case, you do not have a template problem. You have a culture or power problem, and a VP alone will not invent challenge from zero.
