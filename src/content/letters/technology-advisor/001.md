If you run a nonprofit or any org without a CTO, this week looked like a flood of "we will run the whole workflow" tools. Grants platforms, compliance add-ons, AI workspaces. The pitch is capacity. The risk is locked-in scope you cannot read.

You do not need to become technical to survive that. You need a way to tell which line items are load-bearing, which ones are optional packaging, and who answers when the tool and the process disagree after go-live.

The essay is a practical pass on managing a software vendor when there is no CTO in the room, including what to ask before you sign and what to watch in the first ninety days.

> Tip: ask which three line items they would remove if your budget dropped by a quarter. The answer is the negotiation you actually wanted.
