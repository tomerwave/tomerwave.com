When a vendor's quote looks too high, most people push back on the number. That's almost never where the money is.

It's in the scope. Six or seven lines that sound mandatory and aren't, written in a vocabulary that isn't yours, so you can't tell which ones are load-bearing. You negotiate ten percent off the total and keep every line, when you could have removed two of them and saved a third. The vendor isn't hiding anything. They wrote the proposal for someone who'd know what to cut, and you're not that person, and there's no reason you should be.

> Ask the vendor which three line items they'd remove if your budget were cut by a quarter.

They'll tell you. It's a fair question, and the answer is the conversation you actually wanted to have.
