Signing was the easy meeting. Steering is the job.

Without a CTO, vendor relationships drift into slide progress, surprise change orders, and demos that work until real staff touch them on Monday. You do not need jargon to fix that. You need a one-page definition of done, a weekly thirty-minute call with a real environment demo, a decision log you own, access and export clarity, and UAT run by the people who do the work.

This month's essay is a plain-language playbook for the months after the contract, including copy/paste questions and a fourteen-day catch-up if you are already mid-project.

> Tip: "sprint completed" is not an outcome. "Staff can issue a receipt with the right charity number" is. Tie invoices to the second kind.

When the next change order is large or the stories about "done" do not match, a technology assessment is the sharp intervention. The weekly rhythm you can run yourselves.
