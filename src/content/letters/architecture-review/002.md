You can feel ownership getting fuzzy before anyone names it.

Work still ships. Incidents still close. Then a simple question ("who owns billing?") gets three answers, or a shrug, and a week of almost appears on the board. At six engineers the map was ambient. Past ten it has to be written, or it quietly stops being true.

This week's news is full of agents rewriting fleets and recovering thirty-year systems. None of that works if your own surfaces still have three owners and no map.

> If most non-trivial changes touch three "owned" surfaces, you do not have an org-chart problem first. You have a boundary problem wearing an ownership costume.
