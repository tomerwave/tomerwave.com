Every rewrite argument is held over the wrong question. People argue about whether the new system is the right shape, and almost nobody argues about the gap.

The gap is the period where the old system is still running, the new one is not finished, and every change has to be made twice or deferred. It is always longer than the estimate, because the estimate covers building the new system and not the eleven months of running two. Product slows down, and the people who wanted the rewrite are the ones who then get blamed for the slowdown.

> Before approving any rewrite, ask what happens to feature work during the gap, and get the answer in weeks.

If nobody can answer that, the plan is not a plan yet. It is a preference.
